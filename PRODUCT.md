# ClaimChain

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Implemented under the user's end-to-end build authorization: React, TypeScript, Vite, Express, Node 24 SQLite. A single deployable service serves the API and built frontend. AWS integrations use the AWS SDK v3. Local operation requires no cloud credentials.

## Users

Workspace administrators, assigned recovery operators, and read-only auditors helping small stores recover unpaid invoices, assemble document/dispute evidence, and coordinate surplus inventory.

## Product Purpose

Turn evidence into executable recovery workflows. Success means a payment is recorded, an inventory transfer is received, or a reviewed recovery packet is exported, with durable records of what happened.

## Positioning

Evidence, recovery actions, payments, and handoffs belong to one persistent workspace. Every action has an observable result.

## Operating Context

India-focused demonstration, INR amounts, desktop administration and mobile access. Seeded stores, people, documents and transactions are fictional and labeled sample data. This is one role-controlled workspace, not a tenant-isolated marketplace.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Payment cases, evidence uploads, document drafts, task deadlines, payment ledger, stock reservations and transfers, document recovery checklists, case packet export, activity log, workspace settings, individual sessions, three-role RBAC, verification/recovery, security audit, pagination, and synthetic ingestion. User explicitly deferred construction safety; exclude it. AI extraction must identify its provider and require factual review. Legal drafts are templates, not jurisdiction-verified filings. External sending and portal submission are not represented as completed without a provider receipt.

## Brand Commitments

ClaimChain is the working product name from the conversation. The public entry is an exact-black, full-viewport statement with white halftone hands and one green action; the operations workspace continues the black canvas with readable near-black surfaces, dense registers, and restrained status colors. Professional, exceptionally polished frontend requested. The landing artwork source and transformation are recorded beside the asset.

## Evidence on Hand

Empty workspace. No AWS credentials, real invoices, customers, deployments or external service connections have been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Persist changes before reporting success.
- Trace facts back to original evidence.
- Make the next action clear.
- Keep financial and stock calculations deterministic.
- Expose unavailable integrations honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable controls, meaningful labels, visible focus, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts. Target WCAG AA contrast.
